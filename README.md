## Personal Statement on AI Ethics

Chapter 5 of *Ethical Machines* highlights a crucial shift in how we approach organizational ethics by moving away from abstract philosophical guidelines and toward a rigorous operational framework. This means managing ethical risk with the same level of discipline traditionally applied to financial or cybersecurity compliance.

From my perspective as a computer science and cybersecurity student, building secure and dependable software systems means going beyond basic checkboxes and reactive compliance. Real alignment takes actual structural accountability, which means building risk management right into the engineering process from the start. Technical strength and ethical responsibility go hand in hand, so engineers need to design for accountability rather than treating it like an afterthought.
